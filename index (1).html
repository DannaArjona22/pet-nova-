Frequently asked questions

Is Pet Nova free?add

Yes. You can create your account, add your pets and use the main functions for free.

Can I add more than one pet?add

Of course! Each pet gets its own profile, medical history, calendar and photo album.

How does Paw Alert work?add

If your pet gets lost, you activate the alert. People of the Pet Nova community near you get a notification with your pet's photo and last seen location.

Is my information safe?add

We never sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy to see what we collect and how we use it.
